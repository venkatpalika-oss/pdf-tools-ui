Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

PaperlyTools respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our services.

Files are automatically deleted after processing.

Cookies may be used to improve website functionality and analytics. They do not contain uploaded document content.

All file transfers are encrypted using HTTPS. We use secure cloud infrastructure and automated deletion systems to protect user data.
