Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

PaperlyTools respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our services.

File Processing

Documents you upload are transmitted to our server infrastructure and written to temporary storage for the duration of processing. Processing is automated.

We do not operate a document archive, and uploaded files are not added to any user-facing storage. Temporary working files are removed when an operation completes. We cannot, however, guarantee immediate deletion in every case: if an operation fails or is interrupted, a temporary file may persist until it is cleared by routine maintenance or by the ordinary restart cycle of the processing environment.

Some operations send document content to third-party processing services, which apply their own data-handling terms. You should not upload documents containing information you are not permitted to share with a third-party service.

Information We Collect

  • Account details, where you create an account
  • Counts of operations performed, used to apply plan limits
  • Server logs generated in the ordinary course of operating the service

This information is used to operate the service, apply usage limits, and diagnose faults.

Browser Storage and Cookies

Where you sign in, we store a session token in your browser so you remain signed in. Browser storage is also used to record basic usage counts. Neither contains the content of uploaded documents.

Data Transmission

Connections to this website and to our processing endpoints use HTTPS. Access to processing endpoints is restricted to this website's origin and subject to rate limits.

We do not claim any specific security certification, and this policy describes current practice rather than a guarantee of outcome.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements or regulatory changes.
